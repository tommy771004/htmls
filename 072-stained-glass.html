The Lancet of Chancea window leaded by nearest neighbour

Every shard in this window belongs to whichever seed lies nearest to it. Mathematicians call the result a Voronoi diagram; the glaziers of Chartres had no word for it, and needed none. They simply cut the glass where two claims met, and ran lead along the quarrel.

Here the border, the three rosettes and the ground are all cut by the same rule. Only the seeds are placed with intent; the lead finds its own way.
