Cue Sheet

    From the Title Dept.

    We were told the picture is about a man who loses a day of the week. So the titles lose one too: watch the calendar in card four. Every shape on screen was cut from construction paper with dull scissors, on purpose — the wobble is the point. Straight edges are for accountants.

    Three colours only. Mustard for suspicion, black for everything else, and cream because the lab ran out of white. The red is a mistake we decided to keep.

    Studio note, in pencil: “Can the eye blink less? It unsettles the executives.” Our reply, in ink: no.

    Approved · Not for Tuesday
