        About ARC

        ARC is a concept for a calmer brokerage: fewer screens, costs you can see before you tap, and every number where your eye already is.

        What is real here

        The interface. Every control on this page works: orders fill, limits wait for their price, the planner recomputes and sign-up validates. It is one HTML file with no server behind it.

        What is simulated

        • Prices, indices and volumes are generated in your browser.
        • Orders never leave the page. Cash is practice money.
        • Your demo portfolio is saved only in this browser.

        Disclosures

        ARC is not a broker-dealer and offers no securities, advice or accounts. Company names and tickers are used only to label sample data. Projections are hypothetical.
