aber besser.少，但更好。

A calculator that does twenty-four things and pretends to do none of them. Every key is a circle, every circle is the same size, and only one of them is allowed a colour. 二十四顆一樣大的圓鍵，只有一顆有顏色——因為你只需要知道一件事：答案在哪裡。它能用，請直接按。

    An homage to the ET 66 pocket calculator (1987) and to Dieter Rams’ ten principles for good design. Fictional brand; the principles are his. 做得越少，越難。
