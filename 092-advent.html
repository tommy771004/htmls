Twenty-Four Small Doors

A printed advent calendar in the old manner: one door a day until Christmas Eve. It is not December. Open them anyway — nobody from the calendar authority is watching.

Welcome. Tap a door. Each one hides a small, slightly unreliable gift. The snow gets heavier the more you open.
